About

L.E.A. is a Dublin‑based contemporary jazz trio blending tradition with modern edge. Leon Laisné (electric & upright bass) shapes melodic, driving lines; Arthur Noswitz (guitar) brings a fierce touch rooted in the blues; and Eoghan McCloskey (drums) anchors the trio with tight, inventive grooves.
